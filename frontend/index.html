About FoodRescue

FoodRescue is a food donation platform that links people with surplus food to the charities, shelters and community groups that can put it to good use.

Donors post what they have, receivers see what is available nearby, and every collection is tracked from listing to handover.

How it works

  1. 1

    Donate

    Donors post surplus food with pickup windows and storage details.

  2. 2

    Match

    Receivers see scored matches based on zone, category and capacity.

  3. 3

    Collect

    Receivers claim food and arrange collection within the pickup window.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Claimed food is tracked through collection and delivery.

Our Mission

Less good food wasted, and more people fed with dignity.

Donate food

Suitable donations include surplus cooked meals, fresh produce, bakery items and sealed packaged goods that are still within date.

Always record storage type and allergens accurately. Read our food safety guidance before posting a donation.
